Network:
>> A network is a simply system that connects computers, devices, or servers so they can communicate with each other.
>> Think of it like a highway system for data.
>> Computers send and receive information through network, just like cars carry peoples and goods on roads.

Examples:
>> Your home wifi connects to your phone,laptop and smart tv to the internet.
>> Banks connect ATMs and branch offices to the central servers.
>> Social media apps connect millions of users so messages,photos and videos can travel instantly.

Importance of Networking:

>> Web development: Websites and APIs work only because of networks (HTTP requests, servers).
>> App development: Apps like WhatsApp, Zoom, and Gmail rely on networks.
>> Data transfer & communication: Sending files, emails, or real-time chat.
>> Security: Understanding networking helps protect apps from hackers.
>> Performance optimization: Knowing how data travels helps make apps faster.

Simple logic to Understand:

my computer : person
network : road
server/website : shop/office 
data : letters/packages 
>> So, networking is how your computer sends and receives “letters” to/from other computers.
